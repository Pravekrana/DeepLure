# Corrected v2 errors and limitations
The color-augmented trained model and frozen grayscale baseline each retrieved the matching original for all 45 synthetic recoloring queries. Frozen RGB missed two; the no-color ablation missed one. The trained model therefore ties grayscale on primary Recall@1/mAP rather than establishing a win over both baselines. Results from the invalidated v1 split must not be substituted here; see CORRECTION_LOG.md.

At threshold 0.72021425, the trained model accepts 45 positive pairs and 13 other-instance negatives: 0 false negatives and 1,967 true negatives. Precision is 0.77586, recall 1.0, F1 0.87379, despite ROC-AUC approximately 0.9999. Frozen grayscale also accepts 45 positives and 13 negatives at its own validation threshold; its primary F1 is identical. Frozen RGB accepts 119 negatives. The class distribution is 2.22% positive; maximizing validation balanced accuracy does not guarantee high test precision. No thresholds were changed after the corrected test.

AI-assisted private inspection of the three highest-scoring accepted other-instance pairs found two comparisons of folded bundles on white backgrounds with visibly different repeated motifs. Their similar product composition offers a plausible background/layout shortcut. A third pair has related geometric fabric patterns and folds; exact design identity is unresolved, so it may be a false negative in the instance protocol rather than a genuine design-verification error. No human review is claimed, and no post-test relabeling is used to improve scores. Images remain private.

On secondary grayscale and inversion tests, the trained model retains perfect retrieval but rejects one true synthetic pair in each under its unchanged primary threshold. This illustrates why successful ranking does not imply a universally calibrated pair decision. Near-perfect same-photograph retrieval across these controlled transforms is weak evidence about independently photographed or manufactured colorways.

Remaining risks include unknown shared designs across instances, false-negative contrastive pairs, imperfect duplicate/capture screening, conservative false unions, source stretch, cloth/background shortcuts, and small motifs or borders lost at 224 pixels. The duplicate-screening correction and previous exposure further limit holdout claims. Natural same-design verification, real cross-colorway retrieval and verified different-design/similar-palette hard-negative metrics remain unavailable. No rare-event FPR or uncertainty claims are made.

Next evidence should be an authorized, independently annotated collection of exact designs with genuine alternate colorways and distinct captures, including the unresolved geometric candidate pair for separate annotation. Freeze a new protocol before further fitting. Higher resolution, local patches or segmentation are hypotheses to validate, not implemented features claimed here.
