# Audit correction and prior test exposure
The initial v1 experiment used filename lineage, exact hashes and dHash Hamming distance ≤4 to group likely duplicate captures. After its locked test was evaluated, a final private augmentation inspection showed two almost identical training photographs. Their dHash distance was 6 despite tiny pixel differences. A complementary 32×32 RGB check found three pairs among retained representatives: a training/training near-duplicate, a validation/test near-duplicate, and a training/test pair depicting the same chair/background with different textile appearance. AI-assisted inspection confirmed these visual relationships; no human review is claimed.

V1 metrics and artifacts are preserved privately under `private/run` and the original manifest/split paths but are **superseded**. They must not be cited as leakage-screened final performance. The review did not tune an architecture or threshold using a test score; it found a preprocessing/audit deficiency that made the prior protocol unreliable.

The corrected audit groups candidates when either dHash distance ≤4 or mean absolute RGB difference ≤0.025 after bilinear resizing to 32×32. This deliberately conservative extra test catches mild resampling/noise differences and similar common render backgrounds. It can overmerge low-detail images, and it remains a screening heuristic, not identity truth. The visually identified furniture source variants are explicitly excluded as non-saree imagery, with private AI-assisted provenance.

A new v2 manifest and split are frozen. All four model variants use the same seed, initialization, optimizer, augmentations, epoch budget, checkpoint selection rule, metric definitions and validation threshold rule as before. Trained models start again from the ImageNet checkpoint; old trained weights are not reused. The final v2 test is evaluated once after a new lock. No model/threshold adaptation follows it.

**The corpus has been inspected and evaluated before this correction.** The corrected holdout is not pristine never-before-seen data; numerical claims must retain this limitation. Obtaining new independently annotated natural captures/colorways is still necessary for a definitive design-recognition claim. This correction does not turn synthetic results into natural evidence.
