# Interview study guide
This is material to study and verify, not a first-person claim about the applicant's work or understanding.

**Identity vs category.** Two Banarasi fabrics can have entirely different motifs. The four Kaggle categories cannot supervise exact identity. Matching includes motif outlines, internal structure, repeat layout, and visible borders/pallu. Product IDs and colorway names require evidence before becoming labels.

**What was actually learned?** A 256-D projection and the MobileNetV3-Small backbone were trained with two independently augmented views per public-source image. These positives share a photograph; they are not independent manufactured colorways. Vendor repeated views were audited and excluded because capture independence was unresolved. Current evidence supports an instance-matching stress experiment, not proven real design recognition.

**Model path.** `model.py`: 576 pooled backbone features, linear 256-D head, unit-length embedding. Frozen RGB/gray baselines omit that head. `transforms.py`: EXIF→RGB→aspect-fit/pad→ImageNet normalization. Training color jitter and random grayscale discourage palette shortcuts, but cannot guarantee arbitrary palette invariance. Geometry uses small translations without arbitrary crop/mirror.

**Loss and sampling.** For anchor i, average `-log(exp(z_i·z_p/tau)/sum_{a != i} exp(z_i·z_a/tau))` over positive views p. Self-comparisons are excluded. Instance mode assigns a temporary identity to the original; hidden same-design images can be false negatives. Supervised mode requires trusted identities and a P×K sampler of distinct captures; singletons/insufficient identities fail explicitly. No uncertain hard-negative mining is used.

**Splits and leakage.** `data.py` unions hash duplicates, decoded-pixel duplicates, documented source derivatives, near-duplicate candidates (dHash and pixel MAE), supported capture groups and supported design IDs before assigning outer partitions. dHash is a screening device, never identity truth. In a natural benchmark, query/gallery must share held-out design IDs but must not share captures/derivatives. Current synthetic queries intentionally recolor gallery originals and are labelled accordingly. Conservative unions sacrifice coverage; imperfect recall can still leave hidden overlap.

**Training decisions.** Fixed seed, AdamW, one head warm-up epoch then fine-tuning, fixed batch-normalization running statistics for small batches. The selected epoch maximizes validation mAP, earliest on ties. Color ablation changes one configuration field. See actual logs/report; do not assume the trained system beats both baselines.

**Metrics and threshold.** Image-level Recall@K, AP and mAP use cosine ranking. AP is the mean precision at relevant ranks divided by the total number of relevant gallery images. Pair threshold maximizes validation balanced accuracy; highest threshold breaks ties. Test uses the frozen value. ROC-AUC is threshold-independent ranking quality; precision depends on pair prevalence. Cosine similarity is not a probability. Pair threshold is not an open-set gallery threshold. No tiny-sample low-FPR reliability claim is defensible.

**Failure modes.** Natural cross-colorway accuracy remains unknown. Global pooling can lose layout; resizing can erase tiny texture; source stretch distorts motifs; cloth folds/backgrounds can become shortcuts. Grayscale destroys isoluminant boundaries and retains luminance changes. Channel permutations are controlled synthetic tests, not manufactured dyes, illumination changes or independent photography. Use aggregate results and private error evidence to discuss actual failures.

**Compute.** The inference model excludes MobileNet's classifier. Encoder latency, preprocessing and gallery search are timed separately with warm-up/repeats. Embeddings cost 256×4 bytes each; frozen baselines cost 576×4. Exact matrix search is appropriate at this gallery scale. FLOPs were not measured. Private checkpoint size may include optimizer state and differs from inference weights alone.

**What would improve evidence next?** Obtain authorized exact-design and colorway annotations plus independent captures for held-out identities. Include different motifs sharing a palette and true same-design alternate palettes. Validate local patches/higher resolution or garment segmentation only if small-motif/background failures justify their extra cost. For other garments, revisit layout equivalence (sleeves, collars, print placement), collect category-specific captures and recalibrate; do not assume automatic transfer.

**Practice.** Read the contrastive loss, hand-calculate the AP unit test, trace an image through train/inference, reproduce the cache mismatch error, inspect validation-vs-test separation, and explain the limitations without inventing human review. AI tools used: Yes.

**Audit correction to explain.** A final visual check found near-identical images beyond the dHash cutoff, including a validation/test pair. V1 results were invalidated; a complementary pixel screen and explicit furniture exclusions rebuilt the splits, and unchanged models were retrained from ImageNet. V2 is corrected software evidence but not a pristine never-seen holdout because the corpus had already been inspected. Be candid about this and the need for new natural annotations.
